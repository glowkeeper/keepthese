# Literary journeys

## Editorial contract

Literary journeys are finite, ordered invitations through the verified passage
shelf. They connect works without classifying the whole collection, prescribing
an interpretation, choosing words, or manufacturing a reason to return. A
journey contains three to six unique passages and records:

- a stable identifier and schema version;
- a short public title and invitation;
- ordered passage membership;
- an internal editorial rationale for the sequence.

The public client receives the title, invitation, and ordered passage IDs. The
internal rationale remains repository-side. Journey metadata is original
curated metadata covered by the intended CC BY 4.0 treatment in
`LICENSING.md`; source passages and facts are not claimed as project-authored
work.

## Initial journeys

### Thresholds and departures

1. Lewis Carroll, *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*
2. Charlotte Brontë, *Jane Eyre: An Autobiography*
3. Elizabeth Gaskell, *North and South*
4. James Joyce, *Dubliners* — “Eveline”
5. H. G. Wells, *The Time Machine*

The sequence begins with curiosity, moves through an imagined departure and an
unfamiliar arrival, pauses before a difficult leaving, then crosses the
ordinary boundary of time.

### Light and hidden worlds

1. Virginia Woolf, *Mrs. Dalloway*
2. Willa Cather, *My Ántonia*
3. Sarah Orne Jewett, *The Country of the Pointed Firs*
4. Charlotte Perkins Gilman, *The Yellow Wallpaper*
5. George Eliot, *Middlemarch*

The sequence moves from a London morning through open prairie and coastal
village into a confined room, ending with a reflection on the limits of human
attention.

### Divided and becoming

1. Jane Austen, *Persuasion*
2. Frederick Douglass, *Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass, an
   American Slave*
3. Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley, *Frankenstein; Or, The Modern Prometheus*
4. Kate Chopin, *The Awakening*
5. Robert Louis Stevenson, *Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde*

The sequence moves from reflection into purposeful learning, dangerous
creation, emerging awareness, and explicit division. Autobiography and fiction
remain distinct even as the order invites conversation between them.

## Boundaries

The initial three journeys use fifteen of the twenty passages. Omission is not
a judgement of quality, and membership is not an exhaustive thematic label.
The complete shelf remains available one page at a time.

On each journey page, the maker may explicitly keep their poem and continue.
Once all five have been kept, the path resolves into an ordered sequence of the
maker's poems with routes back to revise each one. This completion is a quiet
creative conclusion, not a reward or measure of achievement. The poems retain
their existing passage-specific browser autosaves; the journey itself adds no
durable progress record or personal archive. Encountering earlier saved work is
made explicit, and the maker may continue with it or start that page again.

The complete sequence can be downloaded as one portrait PNG containing its
journey title and all five worked source pages in order. Each page preserves the
maker's chosen material and shows the poem emerging where the other words have
fallen away, with full source attribution and a restrained Keep These credit.
Rendering and download happen entirely on the maker's device. Individual
worked-source PNGs remain available from each poem's making surface.

Journeys add no search, recommendation engine, streak, urgency, popularity
signal, remote request, or automatic judgement of whether a poem is complete.

Maintainer making and editorial review remain required before these sequences
are accepted. The record states maintainer judgement and must not claim that
the journeys represent broad reader preference.
