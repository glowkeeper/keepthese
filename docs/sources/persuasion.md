# Persuasion passage record

## Selection

The initial collection includes the short reflective paragraph in Chapter IV
of Jane Austen's *Persuasion* beginning “How eloquent could Anne Elliot have
been” and ending “an unnatural beginning.”

The passage turns attachment, confidence, caution, prudence, youth, and romance
against one another in a compact space. Those tensions offer several possible
poetic paths without requiring the maker to know Anne Elliot's history.
